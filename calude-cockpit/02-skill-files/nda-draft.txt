(Fictional practice document for the Claude Code course. Not a real contract.)

Parties: Orbit Systems Ltd. ("Orbit") and Greenleaf Office Supplies Ltd. ("Greenleaf").

1. Purpose. The parties wish to discuss a possible supply agreement for office furniture.

2. Confidential Information. Any information shared by Orbit, in any form, is Confidential Information.
   Information shared by Greenleaf is Confidential Information only if it is marked "Confidential" in writing.

3. Term. This agreement lasts ten (10) years and renews automatically for further five-year periods
   unless Orbit gives written notice.

4. Non-solicitation. For five (5) years, Greenleaf will not hire or contact any Orbit employee,
   customer or supplier.

5. Feedback and ideas. Any idea, suggestion or feedback Greenleaf gives Orbit becomes Orbit's property,
   without payment.

6. Liability. Greenleaf is liable for all damages caused by any breach, with no limit.
   Orbit's liability is limited to one hundred US dollars ($100).

7. Return of information. On request, Greenleaf must return or destroy all Confidential Information
   within 24 hours.

8. Governing law. This agreement is governed by the laws of the Cayman Islands. Disputes will be
   heard only in the courts of the Cayman Islands.

9. Entire agreement. This document replaces all earlier discussions between the parties.
