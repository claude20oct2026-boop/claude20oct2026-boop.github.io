Greenleaf Office Supplies - Weekly team meeting
Date: Monday, 2 November 2026
(Fictional practice data for the Claude Code course.)

Attendees: Ronit (CEO), Avi (operations), Galit (customer service), Ben (sales)

1. October sales
Ben: chair sales were strong, up compared to last October. Paper sales were flat.
Ben thinks the new bulk discount helped with chairs.

2. Late deliveries
Avi: our delivery partner was short on drivers for most of October.
About one in four orders arrived late.
Galit: late deliveries are the top reason customers contact us right now.
Ronit: this is now our biggest risk. Two business customers have said they may leave.

3. Wrong items in orders
Galit: several customers got the wrong model or colour.
Avi thinks the cause is two products with very similar codes in the warehouse system.

4. Customer service response times
Galit: email replies take up to four days. The team is two people for about 300 messages a week.
Idea: ready-made answers for the ten most common questions.

Decisions
- Avi will meet a second delivery company this week.
- Galit will draft ready-made answers for the most common questions.
- Ben will pause the paper promotion until deliveries are on time again.

Action items
- Avi: report on the second delivery company by 9 November.
- Galit: first ten ready-made answers by 12 November.
- Avi: rename the two similar product codes in the warehouse system.
- Ronit: call the two unhappy business customers this week.
