Practice files for "Connectors" (lesson one, topic 4).
Everything here is fictional: Greenleaf Office Supplies is a made-up company.

Open this folder in VS Code, then follow the steps on each connector card.
Most examples also use your own accounts (Gmail, Notion, Canva and so on).
Start with examples that only read. Check before Claude sends, posts or buys anything.

customer-feedback.txt   15 customer comments from October
meeting-notes.txt       notes from the weekly team meeting, with action items
sales.csv               12 months of sales: 3 products, 2 regions
products.csv            Greenleaf's 3 products with prices
sale-announcement.txt   the text for the November chair sale
nda-draft.txt           an NDA draft from a supplier, with some one-sided clauses
greenleaf-logo.png      the company logo
photos/                 3 product photos (chair, desk, notebooks)

Photos are from Unsplash (unsplash.com) and free to use.
